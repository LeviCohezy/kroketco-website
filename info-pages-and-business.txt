Section 1: General company information
When was the company officially founded? January 1, 1996


Can you provide a brief history of the company?
In 1996, Lieven Vinckier took over Mr. Roets' small croquette company. Back then, potatoes were still cooked in a small pot on a gas stove and croquettes were made like grandmother did with a mille-croquettes, all in a small shed belonging to father Noël Vinckier. This gradually changed to small machines to produce the croquettes. After a few years, cheese and shrimp croquettes were also produced in-house.
In 2008, the company moved to a new facility in Roeselare.
In 2012, Lieven took over the current Superkroket from Erik Termote and Lieva Debaets. This added a second type of croquette made in a slightly different way. In 2023, alongside the various types of croquettes, fresh mashed potatoes were launched, which we continue to reinvent today in different flavors, sizes, and packaging.

Where is the company located and in which locations are you active? Roeselare industrial zone near Mammoet. Regina Wautersweg 2
Can you tell us more about the production facilities or factory? We are a modern food processing company constantly striving for top quality and hygiene.

What sets Kroketco apart from the competition? What makes you unique?
Only the best is good enough. We start with quality products and end with quality products.
No chemical additives, pure nature. Real potatoes, grey shrimp, local cheeses from Groendal.
To what extent do you use local products or suppliers?
Groendal - cheese and butter are truly local.

rafina 
wholesaler of fresh food products
Rafina is an established Belgian fresh food wholesaler from Lauwe, active since 1954. The company supplies a very broad range to customers including butchers, cheese shops, caterers, and other fresh food stores. Today, the offering comprises more than 7,000 products, including deli meats, cheeses, salads, prepared meals, frozen items, and other fresh produce.
Rafina sets itself apart primarily through own transportation, a dedicated sales representative per customer, an extensive purchasing department, and a strong focus on local and artisanal products. In recent years, the company grew further through acquisitions such as Triporteur in 2017 and ARCA and Deketelaere-Maes Viswaren in 2024.

duva fruit
Duva Fruit NV is a West-Flemish wholesaler in fresh vegetables, fruit, and related food products, based in Gistel. The company was founded in 1992 and currently supplies hundreds of food professionals in West and East Flanders.
Duva Fruit focuses primarily on hospitality, butcher shops, caterers, institutional kitchens, bakeries, catering, and fresh food stores. In addition to fruit and vegetables, it offers a broad complementary assortment including pre-cut vegetables, potato products, mashed potatoes, croquettes, nuts, dried fruit, fruit juices, sprouts, and microgreens.
A key differentiator is the strong logistics service: Duva operates with its own refrigerated transport and makes hundreds of daily deliveries in the region. For hospitality clients, same-day delivery is also available.

vlaemynck natuurlijk
Vlaemynck Natuurlijk is a family business and professional wholesaler in fresh vegetables, fruit, and potato products, operating out of Veurne. The company now spans four generations and supplies professional kitchens across West and East Flanders.
Vlaemynck combines a broad fresh product range with an in-house cutting department and dedicated fleet. They supply clients including hospitality, institutional kitchens, healthcare facilities, caterers, and food businesses, offering processed potatoes, herbs, juices, nuts, cooked vegetables, and other complementary items alongside fruit and vegetables.
A key asset is their fast and flexible logistics: customers can place orders late, and Vlaemynck handles daily deliveries using their own transport. Furthermore, the company operates according to IFS quality standards.

biervliet freez center 
wholesaler of fresh and frozen food products
Biervliet Freez Center Bvba is a West-Flemish food wholesaler based in Diksmuide, specializing in frozen products and a broad assortment for commercial clients. They cater to hospitality, butchers, caterers, bakeries, institutional kitchens, catering services, associations, and businesses.
The product offering is extensive and includes frozen vegetables and fruit, fries, potato products, deep-fried snacks, artisanal croquettes, meat, poultry, fish, desserts, ice cream, and dry goods.
A major strength is their strong regional distribution. Freez Center delivers daily across the Belgian coast and West Flanders, backed by a quick ordering and delivery process. The company is also affiliated with AZ Food Horecagrossiers.
